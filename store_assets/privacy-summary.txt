RangeKeeper EV keeps your range tracking data local.

- No vehicle/range data collected by the developer
- No account required
- No backend or cloud sync for range data
- No app analytics
- Ads served by Google AdMob
- AdMob may process ad-related device, advertising, diagnostics, and interaction data
- Vehicle profiles and check-ins stored locally on your device
- Notification permission used only for optional local daily reminders
- Delete all data via Settings > Reset All Data or by uninstalling the app
