# Feature Graphic Notes - RangeKeeper EV

## Google Play Feature Graphic Specifications

- Size: 1024 x 500 pixels
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- No transparency

## Design Direction

Background: Dark navy or deep charcoal (#0D1B2A or #162032)

Primary accent color: Electric teal/green (#00E5C3)

Secondary accent: Electric green (#39FF14)

## Suggested Visual Elements

- Clean EV silhouette (side view, minimal line art)
- Battery icon with partial or full charge indicator
- Circular range gauge or arc showing range percentage
- Simple upward or downward trend line (graph element)
- App name text: "RangeKeeper EV" in white or teal
- Tagline (optional): "Track your EV range. Understand the trend."

## Typography

- App name: Bold, clean sans-serif, white or electric teal
- Tagline: Regular weight, soft white or gray

## Tone

Premium, minimal, and easy to read. EV-focused utility aesthetic. Dark background with bright teal and green accents. Avoid clutter. No battery health or diagnostic messaging.

## Do Not Include

- Battery health claims
- Diagnostic claims
- Manufacturer logos or trademarks
- Medical or warning symbols
- Complex charts or data visualizations
