# Privacy Policy - RangeKeeper EV

**App:** RangeKeeper EV
**Developer:** Old Alex Hub
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Contact:** Please use the Google Play app listing contact options.

---

## Summary

RangeKeeper EV is a private, offline EV range tracking app. It stores all data locally on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not require an account and has no backend server.

---

## Data Collection

RangeKeeper EV does not collect any personal information. The developer (Old Alex Hub) does not receive any data from users of this app.

---

## Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

- Vehicle profiles (nickname, make, model, year, rated range, optional notes)
- Range check-in entries (battery percentage, displayed range, optional odometer, temperature, notes)
- App settings (preferred range unit, theme preference)
- Reminder preferences (whether reminders are enabled, reminder time)

This data is stored using React Native AsyncStorage on your device. It is not transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

---

## Internet Usage

RangeKeeper EV does not require an internet connection. The app does not make any network requests. No data is sent to or received from any server.

---

## Permissions

RangeKeeper EV requests the following permissions:

- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: Used only for optional local daily reminders. The notification reminds you to log your vehicle's range. This permission is requested only when you choose to enable daily reminders in Settings. You can disable reminders at any time.
- **VIBRATE**: Used to allow notification vibration for reminders.
- **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED**: Used to reschedule local reminders after the device restarts.
- **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM**: Used to schedule local reminders at the time you choose.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone state, SMS, Bluetooth, or any account permissions.

---

## Notifications

Notifications are local and generated entirely on your device. They are not pushed from a remote server. They contain only the text you see in the reminder, using your vehicle's nickname. No notification data is transmitted externally.

---

## No Account

RangeKeeper EV does not require or support user accounts. There is no registration, login, email, phone number, or password involved.

---

## No Backend

RangeKeeper EV has no backend server. All calculations, storage, exports, forecasting, and notifications run locally on your device.

---

## No Analytics

RangeKeeper EV does not include analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.

---

## No Advertising

RangeKeeper EV does not display ads and does not use any advertising SDKs.

---

## No Third-Party Sharing

RangeKeeper EV does not share any data with third parties because no data leaves your device.

---

## Children's Privacy

RangeKeeper EV does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect any personal data from any user regardless of age.

---

## Data Deletion

To delete all your data:

1. Open the app and go to Settings.
2. Tap "Reset All Data" and confirm.

Alternatively, uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

---

## Export

The export feature in the Reports screen shares your locally stored data using your device's share sheet. Data is only shared to apps or destinations you choose. The developer does not receive exported data.

---

## App Disclaimer

RangeKeeper EV is not a battery diagnostic tool and does not measure actual battery capacity. Estimates are based on manually entered range and battery percentage data. Actual EV range may vary due to temperature, driving style, terrain, tire condition, charging habits, vehicle software, and manufacturer range calculation methods. This app is for informational use only. It does not replace professional vehicle inspection.
